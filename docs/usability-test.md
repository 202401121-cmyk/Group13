# Usability test: can a shopper understand a result without help? (US-33, by-hand job 8)

US-33 asks that 4 of 5 non-technical testers understand a result without help. This page is the whole test: who
to ask, what to say, what to watch for, and the table to fill in. Allow about 20 minutes per tester.

## Testers

- **Five people,** each tested alone. None may study or work in computing, and none may have used ShopSense before.
  Friends and family are fine.
- **Ask first:** may we watch, and take notes with no names? Write each tester only as T1 to T5.
- **Setup:** use the staging site on the tester's own phone, or on a laptop they are used to. Assistive technology
  they already use (a screen reader, zoom, a bigger text size) is welcome. Note which one.

## The task

Read this out once, word for word, then say nothing more about the app:

> "Please use this website to find the best phone for you, for your own needs and budget. When you have a result,
> tell me in your own words which phone it suggests, and why it fits you."

The tester types their own request; we never suggest one. Start a timer when they open the site.

## While they work

- **Stay quiet.** If they ask a question, say "What would you do if I weren't here?" Only step in if the test can't go
  on, such as when the site is down. Mark that as help.
- **Note** each place they stop for more than about 10 seconds, re-read something aloud, or look for a word they
  don't know. Write down that word.

## What counts as understood without help

A tester **understood** the result when all three are true, with no help from us:

1. They name the product shown first (the best match), or say plainly that nothing matched and why.
2. They give at least one reason it fits them, in their own words, that matches a reason on the page.
3. They say one thing they would check before buying, or where the page shows its sources. For example: a trade-off,
   a value we couldn't check, or a price that may have changed.

Any help from us, or 2 of the 3 at most, means **not understood**. The US-33 target is met when 4 or more of the 5
testers understood.

## Afterwards

- Copy the words testers stopped at into the US-33 story as gaps, each with the page it was on.
- If the target is not met, fix the plainest gap first and test again with five new testers. Never re-test the same
  people: they have learned the app.
