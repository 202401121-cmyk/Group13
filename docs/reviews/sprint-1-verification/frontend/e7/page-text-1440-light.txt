Privacy

ShopSense is a student project. Here is exactly what it keeps when you search.

What we save

When you send a request, the ShopSense server saves one record with:

what you typed, exactly as you typed it;
a session ID: a random code that links the steps of one search;
what we understood (the product, what it's for, your budget, must-haves and things to leave out), once it was clear enough to research;
whether our AI or our simple keyword rules worked that out;
when it was saved.

We use these records to learn what people really ask for and to test ShopSense. Only the ShopSense server can read them.

What we don't save

You don't need an account. We never ask for your name, email address or a password, and we don't save your IP address or which browser you use.

While you search

The server keeps your answers to our questions in its memory, not in the database. They are deleted 2 hours after your last step.

To understand your request, we may send it to an AI service that does not use it to train its models. We never send it to one that might.

In your browser

Your brief and your answers are kept in this browser tab, so reloading the page doesn't lose them. They are gone when you close the tab. Your light or dark theme choice is kept in this browser.
